How to play

On foot

  • W A S D walk · Shift run · Space jump · Mouse look
  • Left click swing hammer
  • Q / Right click pick up blocks (hold)
  • G throw (auto-aims into the crusher hopper)
  • E use: drive a machine, fill / pour your bucket, open the mixer
  • B spawn menu · M multiplayer · H help

Excavators

  • W S drive · A D steer
  • ← → swing · ↑ ↓ boom
  • R / F arm out / in · Z / X curl / dump
  • Click / Space grapple, breaker on/off, skip gate
  • C cab camera · E climb out

Dust → cement → road

  • Rubble in the crusher hopper is crushed into dust that keeps the block's colour. It rides the conveyor onto the pile.
  • Scoop dust from the pile (bucket machine, or E with your bucket) and pour it into the mixer.
  • Get water from the lake (AQUAMOLE water tank, any bucket, or your hand bucket) and pour it in too.
  • The paddles mix the colours together into cement. Carry it to the potholes in the street and pour to fix them.
