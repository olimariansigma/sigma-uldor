How to play

On foot

  • W A S D walk · Shift run · Space jump · Mouse look
  • Left click swing hammer
  • Q / Right click pick up blocks (hold)
  • G throw (auto-aims into the crusher hopper)
  • E use: drive a machine, fill / pour your bucket, open the mixer
  • 1 hammer · 2 cement trowel: click on the ground to lay cement
  • B spawn menu · M online · H help

Machines

  • W S drive · A D steer
  • ← → swing · ↑ ↓ boom
  • R / F arm out / in · Z / X curl / dump
  • Click / Space tool: grab, clamp, breaker, gate, crusher, vibration, paving
  • T / Y rotate the BG90 brick clamp
  • Tipper trucks: load the body with a machine, drive it over, then Z tips it and X lowers it
  • Drive or press the arm into a wall to grind it down. Bricks are sticky: keep pushing.
  • C cab camera · E climb out

Dust → cement → road

  • Rubble in the crusher hopper is crushed into dust that keeps the block's colour. It rides the conveyor onto the pile.
  • Put dust in the mixer (button, bucket or a machine) and add any amount of water (hose button or lake water). The paddles turn it into cement.
  • Take cement onto your trowel, then click the ground to lay cement tiles anywhere, or click a pothole to fill it. Machines (skip, paver) work too; roll repaired potholes flat with the roller.
