Q&A

How do I build a house?

How do I build a house, step by step?
  1. Make dust. Knock blocks loose, pick them up with Q and throw them into the crusher hopper with G. The dust rides the conveyor onto the pile.
  2. Press blocks. At the brick press next to the dust pile, switch its feeder belt on at the panel (E), or tip dust into its hopper. New blocks come out on the pallet in the dust's colour.
  3. Mix mortar. Open the mixer (E): add 25 kg of dust from the pile, add water, and let the paddles turn it into cement. Then press Take cement on my trowel.
  4. Carry blocks. Hold Q at the press pallet to pick up pressed blocks.
  5. Put mortar down. Go to the concrete slab across the road. With tool 2 (trowel), each click puts one splash of mortar on the slab or on top of a block.
  6. Lay the block. With tool 4 (build), aim at the mortar and click. R turns the block. On mortar it sticks; without mortar it's only stacked.
  7. Go up row by row. Splash mortar on top of the blocks you laid and keep going. Leave gaps for a door and windows.
  8. Let a machine help. An excavator or the BG90's magnetic clamp can set pressed blocks down on mortar too. Put them down upright and they stick.
In progression mode, what do I need to buy before I can build?
Only the build kit ($600) to lay blocks: the cement trowel for putting mortar down is yours from the start. The crusher, mixer and brick press are free to use. A carry upgrade ($250) helps a lot early on.
Why doesn't my block count?
Only blocks fixed on mortar count. A block stacked without mortar is loose and doesn't count, and neither does one that falls off or gets knocked out. You're paid once for each new house record, so knocking your house down and rebuilding it doesn't pay twice.
How do accidents work?
  1. Every few minutes a plane or a car crashes into a garden wall outside the yard. A red light beam and the GPS arrow at the top of the screen show where.
  2. Take the yard pickup parked by the gate (E to get in) and drive there.
  3. Smash the wreck with the hammer, charges or a machine and load the pieces into the tipper truck parked on site.
  4. Rebuild the hole: E at the mortar tub fills your trowel, the bricks are on the blue tarp. Mortar (2), then lay (4).
  5. Get paid: $6,000 for a plane, $3,500 for a car, +25% if you're done within 8 minutes.
Is it really free? Do I need an account or any API keys?
Yes, it's free. No account, no ads, no API keys, no paid services. The game runs entirely in your browser, and your saves stay on your own device.
Where are my saves?
In your browser's local storage on this device, or in the app on Windows and Android. Progression saves itself; sandbox has an autosave plus five save slots. Clearing your browser data deletes them.
Windows says "unknown publisher" or SmartScreen blocks the launcher
The launcher isn't code-signed (signing certificates cost money), so Windows warns about it. Click More info → Run anyway. If the .exe download is corrupted or blocked, use the .zip version, or just play in the browser.
What are the controls?
W A S D walk, Mouse look, Click swing the hammer, Q pick up, G throw, E drive or use, B spawn menu or shop, H full help. In a machine: W S drive, A D steer, arrow keys swing and boom, R F Z X arm and bucket, Click uses the tool. On phones and tablets there are on-screen controls.
