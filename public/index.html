Controls

On foot

  • W A S D walk · Shift run · Space jump
  • Mouse look (click the game to lock the mouse)
  • Left click swing hammer
  • Q / Right click pick up rubble (hold)
  • G throw rubble (aims at the hopper when you're close)
  • E enter the excavator
  • B shop · H help

Excavator

  • W S tracks forward / back · A D steer
  • ← → swing the house · ↑ ↓ boom
  • R / F arm out / in · Z / X curl grapple
  • Left click or Space close / open the grapple
  • C cab camera · E climb out

How to earn

  • Break walls with the hammer, or rip them out with the excavator grapple.
  • Rubble that lands in the crusher hopper is crushed into gravel and sold.
  • Walls fall down when the pieces holding them up are gone, so cut low!
  • Clear 90% of a building to get the job bonus and unlock the next site.
