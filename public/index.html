How to play

On foot

  • W A S D walk · Shift run · Space jump · Mouse look
  • Left click swing hammer
  • Q / Right click pick up blocks (hold)
  • G throw (auto-aims into the crusher hopper)
  • E use: drive a machine, fill / pour your bucket, open the mixer
  • 1 hammer · 2 cement trowel: click to put a small circle of mortar where you aim, or aim at a pothole to fill it
  • 3 demolition charges: click blocks to stick up to 8 charges, then E to blow them (stand back!)
  • 5 remove: scrape off a splash of mortar, or knock a block you laid loose again
  • 4 build: carry pressed blocks, trowel a splash of mortar (one small circle per click, wherever you aim: the slab, the ground or the top of a block), then click to lay a block on it. R turns the block. Blocks laid on mortar stick; without it they are only stacked.
  • P photo mode: hides the HUD and frees the camera. Fly with W A S D + Mouse, Space / C up / down, Shift faster
  • J contracts (progression mode only): three paid jobs at a time. Finish them for money, inside the ⏱ time for +50%. Sandbox has no money at all
  • B spawn menu · M online · H help

Machines

  • W S drive · A D steer
  • ← → swing · ↑ ↓ boom
  • R / F arm out / in · Z / X curl / dump
  • Click / Space tool: grab, clamp, breaker, gate, crusher, vibration, paving
  • BG90: the clamp is magnetic. Close it near blocks and up to 4 snap into place and stay there until you open it. T / Y rotate it
  • Special excavators: Click / Space switches the tool on / off (magnet, vacuum, drums, saw, drill, plate, water jet), locks the forks or lets out the wrecking-ball chain. VX50 blaster: hold T / Y to fire the blocks in its cage. BL4 backhoe: T / Y front loader down / up, Click tips the front bucket
  • Tipper trucks: load the body with a machine, drive it over, then Z tips it and X lowers it. Back up to the intake funnel behind the crusher and tip: the compactor turns the blocks into cubes for the crusher
  • Drive or press the arm into a wall to grind it down. Bricks are sticky: keep pushing.
  • C cab camera · N horn · E climb out

Dust → cement → road

  • Rubble in the crusher hopper is crushed into dust that keeps the block's colour. It rides the conveyor onto the pile.
  • Put dust in the mixer (button, bucket or a machine) and add any amount of water (hose button or lake water). The paddles turn it into cement.
  • Take cement onto your trowel, then click the ground to lay cement tiles anywhere, or click a pothole to fill it. Machines (skip, paver) work too; roll repaired potholes flat with the roller.
  • Brick press (next to the dust pile): switch on its feeder belt at the panel, or tip dust into its hopper. It presses blocks in the dust's colour onto the pallet. Pick them up (Q) and build with tool 4 on the slab across the road.
  • LP20 road painter: T Y pick a colour, Click / Space sprayer on, then drive over a repaired pothole to paint it.
  • Street traffic bounces through every pothole you leave open. Cars stop for you, your machines and anything lying on the road, overtake when they can, and honk when they can't. Settings can switch it off.
  • Weather changes on its own: rain makes the ground wet and shiny, storms bring lightning and thunder. Pick a fixed weather in Settings.

Accidents

  • Every few minutes a plane or a car crashes through a garden wall somewhere outside the yard (sandbox and progression). The GPS arrow at the top of the screen and a red light beam show where.
  • Take the yard pickup (parked near the start, E to get in) and drive there.
  • Smash the wreck apart (hammer, charges or a machine) and load the pieces into the tipper truck parked on site (or drive them to the crusher).
  • Rebuild the hole in the wall: E at the mortar tub fills your trowel for free, the bricks are on the blue tarp. Mortar where each block goes (2), then lay it (4).
  • Done: in progression you get paid (more for quick work). The menu can report one right away or hand one over.

Controller

  • Plug in or pair any gamepad (Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro...) and press a button.
  • On foot: left stick walk (push all the way to run) · right stick look · A jump · B use / drive · X or LT pick up · Y throw · RT hammer / tool · LB RB change tool · D-pad up shop / spawn · D-pad down contracts
  • In a machine: left stick drive · right stick swing / boom · RB LB arm out / in · RT LT curl / dump · A tool · X Y extra control (front loader, clamp rotation...) · B climb out · D-pad left / right camera · D-pad up cab view · D-pad down horn
  • Start menu · Back help · in menus: D-pad moves, A presses, B goes back, LB RB switch tabs

Progression mode

  • You start with only the hammer and $150. Smash the bungalow, pick blocks up (Q) and throw them (G) into the crusher hopper: every kg crushed pays $3.
  • Open the Shop (B): the cement trowel is yours from the start; buy the build kit and charges, upgrade your hammer, carry capacity and crusher, buy excavators and trucks (sell them back for half), and buy new buildings to knock down.
  • Every block you lay in your house pays $30, and house milestones pay bonuses: 20, 50, 100, 180 and 300 blocks. Contracts pay into the same wallet. There are no potholes in this mode, and it saves itself.
  • Co-op: open Online (M), host and give friends the code. They join from their title screen and you all share one wallet, the same tools and the same Shop: anyone can buy, everything anyone crushes or builds pays into it. Only the host keeps the career save and the contracts.

Q&A: how do I build a house?

  1. Make dust. Crush rubble: throw blocks into the crusher hopper. The dust rides the conveyor onto the pile.
  2. Press blocks. Walk to the brick press next to the dust pile and switch its feeder belt on at the panel (E), or tip dust into its hopper. Pressed blocks come out on the pallet in the dust's colour.
  3. Make mortar. Open the mixer (E at the tub): add 25 kg dust from the pile, add water, and wait for the paddles to mix cement. Then Take cement on my trowel.
  4. Pick up blocks. Hold Q at the press pallet to carry blocks (more with the carry upgrade).
  5. Mortar down. Tool 2 (trowel): each click puts a small circle of mortar exactly where you aim, on the slab across the road, the ground or on top of a block. Mistake? Tool 5 (remove) scrapes it off.
  6. Lay it. Tool 4 (build): aim at the mortar and click to set a block there. R turns the block. On mortar it sticks; without mortar it is only stacked and will not count.
  7. Go up. Splash mortar on top of the blocks you laid and keep going, row by row. Leave gaps for doors and windows.
  8. Use machines. An excavator or the BG90 clamp can set pressed blocks onto mortar too: put them down upright and they stick.

Blocks that fall off or get knocked loose stop counting. In progression you are only paid once for each new record, so knocking your house down and rebuilding it does not pay twice.
